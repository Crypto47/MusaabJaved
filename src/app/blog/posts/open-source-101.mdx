# How to get started with making open source contributions?

before i answer that, let me just say that open source was something very remote and terrifying to me a few months back, but i lowkenuinely love being a part of it now.

these tips are based off of my observations of beginner contributors who overlook these basic things and end up ruining the fun for everyone...


here is the list, in order of highest importance (thank me later):

1. read contribution guidelines and the code of conduct before you do anything.
2. make sure you don't skip step 1.
3. join the community to be a part of internal communication. introduce yourself and start interacting with people (don't make irrelevant convos pls).
4. be genuinely curious.
5. read old threads, see where people are getting stuck and how they are approaching the problem at hand.
6. read closed issues and prs, you will get to learn why they were not needed and hence closed 
(this is the best way to gain knowledge when contributing to any os repo imho).
7. be kind and considerate:
    - almost all contributors are volunteers :)
    - contributors collaborate from all around the world (different time zones) so learning patience and asynchronous communication will be your best bet.
    - help newcomers out, you were once in their place too.
8. avoid tagging people or dm-ing them unless very very necessary.
9. search your query before asking a question; there's a high chance your question has already been answered.
10. same goes for issues; search both closed and open issues before opening a new one.
11. be helpful, provide value and everyone will love you.
12. don't blindly push ai slop... be human, make mistakes and learn from them.
