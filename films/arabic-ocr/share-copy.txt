Scanned Arabic archives are indexed and still unsearchable: one lost dot changes a letter, and a scanner that splits a word in two hides it from every search.

I built a 0.5B model that repairs the OCR before it reaches the index. Word error rate falls from 41.1% to 20.4% on 200 held-out segments, it trained in 80 minutes on one laptop GPU, and it runs on CPU, on-premise.

Built with Qwen2.5-0.5B, LoRA and 57k synthetic pairs, because no paired Arabic OCR corpus existed.
