# "hey", "hi bro" or "assalamualaikum" and then dead silence...

be prepared to be left on seen if you only say "hello" and then wait for the other person to reply

the person receiving your message has to think twice before responding to your salutations. 

"is this guy going to waste my time?", "i hope he doesn't ask something stupid.", "just state your business bro..." is what most people think whenever you start a conversation with greetings only.

i know you are just trying to be polite, but leaving the other person hanging and wondering why you have messaged them in the first place is not nice at all as it wastes their time.

so instead of sending:

- "hi",
- "hello",
- "assalamualaikum, how are you?"

just send:

- "hi, how are you? can you tell me when does X happen?",
- "assalamualikum, i was wondering if you could share some learning resources for x and y. thanks!",
- ... etc

this enables the person receiving your message to respond to you as as soon as they are available, instead of having to wait for you to state your business after responding to your initial "hello".

and this refers to what is called **"asynchronous communication"**.

didn't mean to hurt you if you do this, but at least now you know why people often don't respond back to you :)
